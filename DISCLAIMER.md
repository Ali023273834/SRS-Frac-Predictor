# Disclaimer

This software is a **research and validation tool**. It is **not a medical device** and is **not intended for clinical decision-making**.

**What the predictions are based on:**
- a single-institution planning atlas (single-isocentre VMAT, Elekta Versa HD, Monaco);
- a 12-patient OAR cohort.

**What the tool cannot see:**
- cumulative dose after re-irradiation;
- other targets;
- OARs not entered;
- delineation;
- deliverability;
- the patient's clinical situation.

**Status:** the current model (v3, locked 7 November 2026) has been tested on 24 clinical plans of the developing institution only (re-irradiations excluded: the model does not consider prior dose). It has not been validated prospectively or in other institutions.

**Responsibility:** every treatment plan and every choice of fractionation must follow the institutional protocol and be reviewed by qualified medical physicists and radiation oncologists. The authors accept no liability for any use of this software.
